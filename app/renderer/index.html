Auto correction

no reference - saturation, tone and hue are only corrected when clearly wrong
Corrects the transfer, not the art: black / white level, a cast on the whites and on the black line art, a heavy fade and clipped colour. With a reference of the same show it also matches saturation, contrast, midtones, shadow lift, shadow colour and hue. A cast that changes from shot to shot is the art and is left alone. Untick a line to leave that part out.

Colour wheels

drag the dot · Shift = fine
Shadows = lift, Midtones = gamma, Highlights = gain: the dot tints that range toward its colour, the slider under each wheel makes it brighter / darker. Auto colour puts its cast corrections here (Highlights = whites, Shadows = blacks).

Temporary files

      

        

      

        

      
VFR analysis, encode logs and scripts of each export, one sub-folder per source. Default: temp\ in the app folder. The finished videos go to the output folder set in Export.

    

    

      
Updates

      
      

      
One request to GitHub (github.com/EVA0111/EVA-toolkit releases); nothing about you or your files is sent. Installing
        downloads the release zip and copies it over this folder after the app closes - your settings, installed engine
        (runtime\python), engines\, temp\ and your own filters stay. Then the new version starts.

    

    

      
About the two

      
PyTorch runs the models directly: starts at once, works on any CUDA card and on the CPU.

      
TensorRT compiles each model for your GPU the first time it meets a frame size (5–25 s per model, kept in
        engines\ for next time). Same picture to within 1 level; measured on a RTX 5070 Ti: the full clean-up
        chain 7.9 → 15.9 fps, dots + rainbows model 13 → 30 fps. If a model cannot be compiled it runs on PyTorch.
