Analyze

Runs every detector on episode 1 and sets the repairs in Adjust. Once per series is enough.

Telecine / PAL type, dot crawl, rainbows, MPEG blocking, halos, colour shift and bleed. Colour correction and crop are not touched: each has its own Auto button in its step.

Repair

In processing order. Each group has its own Auto button that measures this source for that group only. auto = set by Auto, edited ↺ = changed by you (click to undo).

Auto values are suggestions, measured on a sample of the episode - they can be wrong. Always check the result in the preview (Split, Play) and change what does not look right.

Telecine & interlacing

FieldNet does the IVTC repair itself: it gets the raw field-matched frames and the original fields, and rebuilds combed and unmatched frames on its own. Combing repair, unmatched-frame repair, ICP fix and anti-aliasing are not used with it.

Temporal stabilizer

Built into FieldNet: after it, motion-compensated averaging, fine-detail and still-area smoothing and a long still-area lock run automatically (real Courage disc: door and rug flicker 1.1 -> 0.1, rug lettering shimmer 0.65 -> 0.03), so no separate stabilizer is needed.

Auto correction

no reference - saturation, tone and hue are only corrected when clearly wrong
Corrects the transfer, not the art: black / white level, a cast on the whites and on the black line art, a heavy fade and clipped colour. With a reference of the same show it also matches saturation, contrast, midtones, shadow lift, shadow colour and hue. A cast that changes from shot to shot is the art and is left alone. Untick a line to leave that part out.

Adjustment layers

A grade for part of the video (one scene, a flashback): put layers on the tracks of the timeline under the picture. A layer grades everything under it - the base grade and lower tracks. Click a layer to edit it with the controls above; click it again for the Base grade (whole video).

About the two

      
PyTorch runs the models directly: starts at once, works on any CUDA card and on the CPU.

      
TensorRT compiles each model for your GPU the first time it meets a frame size (5–25 s per model, kept in
        engines\ for next time). Same picture to within 1 level; measured on a RTX 5070 Ti: the full clean-up
        chain 7.9 → 15.9 fps, dots + rainbows model 13 → 30 fps. If a model cannot be compiled it runs on PyTorch.
